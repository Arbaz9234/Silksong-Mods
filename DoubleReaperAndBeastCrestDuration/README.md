# 2x Reaper mode and Beast Rage Effects

Doubles the duration of Reaper and Beast Crest Bind effects. Beast Fury also gains double healing.

## Overview

This mod enhances the Reaper Crest & Beast Crest Bind effects to be twice as powerful, while fully preserving the original mechanics.

## Bind With Reaper Crest

- Hornet enters Reaper mode
- Hitting enemies spawns floating Silk fragments
- The effect lasts 10 seconds and ends upon taking damage

## With This Mod

- Reaper mode duration is doubled.
- Silk fragments continue spawning for twice as long
- Won't reset on taking damage.

## Bind With Beast Crest

- Hornet enters a Fury state
- Fury lasts ~5 seconds
- Hitting enemies heals Masks, up to a maximum of 3 Masks

## With This Mod

- Fury duration is doubled (~10s)
- The maximum number of Masks that can be healed during Fury is doubled [ 3 → 6 Masks or 4 → 8 Masks when Multibinder is equipped]
- The HUD outlines the increased number of healable Masks
