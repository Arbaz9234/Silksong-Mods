# Always have Spider Strings

Permanently equip Spider Strings: the musician charm in Silksong.

## Overview

This mod equips Spider Strings: the musician charm in Silksong. Sophisticated Weaver device. Resonates with music played to amplify and enhance a performance.

## Features

- Your tools permanently have SpiderStrings
- Resonates with music played to amplify and enhance a performance.
- Expands and strengthens the effects of the Needolin.
- It increases the range of the Needolin by 25% and increases the time enemies spend singing by 1.25-2.5 seconds.
