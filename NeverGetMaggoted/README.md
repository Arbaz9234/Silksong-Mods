# Never Maggoted

This mod tweaks how Muckmaggot poison affects Hornet, offering 3 separate versions to suit different playstyles.

## Overview

Adds alternative behaviors to Muckmaggot poison in Hollow Knight: Silksong, letting players heal while cleansing, auto-cleanse on exit, or gain full immunity via three optional versions.

## V1: Heal + Cleanse on Bind

- Hornet still gets infected in the maggot pool.
- Normal silk depletion and vignette effects remain.
- Binding cleanses the infection AND heals normally

Best for: Players who want a fair but less punishing experience.

## V2: Auto-Cleanse on Exit

- Hornet gets infected while inside the maggot pool
- All normal maggot effects apply
- Exiting the pool automatically removes the infection
- No bind required to cleanse

Best for: Players who want environmental danger without lingering punishment.

## V3: Complete Immunity

- Hornet is fully immune to Muckmaggot poison
- No infection, no silk drain, no debuffs
- Muckmaggot pools behave like normal water
